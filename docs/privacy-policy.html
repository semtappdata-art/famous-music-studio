Famous Music Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

Famous Music Studio produces original music and short-form videos, and publishes them to platforms including YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. This policy explains what we collect when we do that, and why.

01What we collect

When we connect a publishing tool to your platform account (for example, to upload a video to YouTube or TikTok on your behalf), the platform issues an authorization token scoped to that specific action — uploading video, reading basic profile info to confirm the right account is connected. We store that token locally to avoid asking you to re-authorize every time.

We do not operate a public-facing app that collects data from viewers or visitors. If you're reading this, you're most likely the studio's own operator, not an end user of a consumer product.

02How we use it

  • To publish rendered videos to the platform account you've connected.
  • To read back basic statistics (views, likes) for videos we've published, so we know what's working.
  • Nothing else. We don't build advertising profiles, and we don't sell or share this data with third parties.

03Third-party platforms

YouTube (Google) — video upload and statistics, via the YouTube Data API. Governed additionally by Google's Privacy Policy.

TikTok — video publishing, via the Content Posting API. Governed additionally by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

04Retention & security

Authorization tokens are kept only as long as needed to keep publishing working, stored on the machine that runs the publishing tool — not in a shared or hosted database. Revoking access from your platform's own account settings (YouTube's or TikTok's security settings) immediately invalidates the token on our end too.

05Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will move and the change will be summarized here. Continued use after a change means you've accepted the update.

Questions about this policy: reach Famous Music Studio through the contact channel listed on our platform profiles.
